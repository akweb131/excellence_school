Academics

A structured curriculum for lifelong learning.

Pre-Primary (Playgroup to Sr KG)

Our early childhood program focuses on learning through play, exploration, and hands-on activities. We emphasize motor skills, language development, and social interaction.

  • Sensory and Motor Development
  • Phonics and Early Literacy
  • Art, Music, and Movement

Primary & Middle (Class I to VIII)

As students progress, the curriculum becomes more structured, balancing academic rigor with creative activities. We follow an English medium syllabus with a focus on core subjects.

  • Core Subjects: English, Math, Science, Social Studies
  • Computer Education & Technology
  • Extracurriculars & Physical Education
